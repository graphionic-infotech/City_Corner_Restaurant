Our Story

The same corner, twenty-six years later.

City Corner has held the same spot opposite Lunsikui Ground for twenty-six years — long enough to feed a generation of Navsari. Known around Lunsikui for its sandwiches and pizza, the kitchen has grown into a full pure-veg card: Punjabi mains, Chinese, biryani, soups and more, while the corner itself hasn’t moved an inch.

Step inside and it’s an easy, colourful dining room — wooden tables, wall murals, neon after dark. Families at dinner, students on a budget, office lunches that run long. Eat in, take the outdoor seats, pick up a takeaway, or order in on Zomato.
